Helencare ERP was an ERP implementation for a manufacturing organization of more than 400 people, delivered while I worked at Lotusa Technology Solutions Joint Stock Company. The implementation took 15 months.

Besides developing and customizing ERPNext, I was responsible for managing the implementation project. The system covered manufacturing, inventory, sales, purchasing, quality management, human resources, CRM and material requirements planning (MRP).

> The 400+ figure is the organization's headcount, not the number of active system users.
